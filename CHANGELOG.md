# Changelog

## 0.4.2

- A cast bar in the window: while the line is out, a bar behind "Line is out" counts down the time left on your cast, with the seconds beside it.
- Enhanced fishing sounds also turn master volume up to full while a pole is equipped. Before, only the sound effects volume went up, so a low master volume kept the splash quiet. Your master volume comes back when the pole comes off.

## 0.4.1

- Sound in the background is now a choice: While a pole is equipped (the default), Always, or Off. If you had it on in 0.4.0, it moves to While a pole is equipped; off stays off.

## 0.4.0

- Every lure in WoW: Forever's game data: the six classic lures plus "Secret" Bait and the Wide-Brimmed Fishing Hat. The hat's lure is used while you wear it and it's off cooldown, and it goes first when lures tie on bonus, because it isn't used up.
- Lure order: biggest bonus your skill can use first; at equal bonus, the hat, then the easier-to-get lure.
- Sound in the background is its own option, on by default, and applies all the time instead of only while a pole is equipped.

## 0.3.0

- Lures on cast: when your pole has no lure, your next double right-click or Cast Fishing key press puts on the best lure in your bags that your skill can use, and the press after that casts. Presses wait while a lure goes on, so they can't interrupt it. With no usable lure in your bags, Bobber just casts. A lure that fails twice in a row is skipped for the session. On by default; turn it off with Put on a lure when you cast.
- The window says when your next cast puts on a lure, and shows a lure going on.
- Shorter window hints, so each fits on one line.

## 0.2.0

- A gear button in the window's header opens Bobber's options.
- A Window opacity option, from 0% (clear) to 100% (solid), defaulting to 90%. It fades the background, header, and border; text and icons stay fully visible. Hold Shift and turn the mouse wheel over the window to change it there.

## 0.1.0

First release, for WoW: Forever (interface 16001).

- Double right-click the water to cast. This works only while a fishing pole is equipped.
- Right-click anywhere to hook and loot while the line is out. You can switch to a double right-click, or turn it off.
- A Cast Fishing key binding that also hooks while the line is out, and equips your pole if it's off.
- Soft targeting is widened while the line is out, so the hook always finds the bobber.
- Fishing-only auto-loot.
- Enhanced fishing sounds while a pole is equipped: sound effects at full volume, music and ambient sound off, and sound in the background. Your settings come back when the pole comes off, after a reload, and at logout.
- Gear slots for your pole and weapons. Drag items in to choose them, or let Bobber remember what you use. The weapon slots work in combat, and your weapons go back on when combat starts.
- A catch window with session, zone, and all-time tabs. It shows each fish's count and share, and counts casts, catches, getaways, early hooks, catches per hour, and fishing time.
- Fishing skill with your pole and lure bonus, skill gained this session, and lure time left.
- A movable, lockable, collapsible window. Ctrl and the mouse wheel scale it. It has four themes: Harbor, Midnight, Ember, and Parchment (light).
